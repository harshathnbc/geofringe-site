See the ground move, millimetre by millimetre.

GeoFringe turns Sentinel-1 satellite radar into point-by-point ground-motion measurements: how fast buildings, roads, pipelines and levees are sinking or rising, with an uncertainty on every number, for engineering, infrastructure and insurance teams.

What you get

A measurement product, not a pretty map: every point carries its velocity, its uncertainty and a time series you can check against your own surveys.

Per-point velocities

Line-of-sight motion in millimetres per year for buildings, infrastructure and open ground, with a one-sigma uncertainty on each point.

Full history

Displacement time series from 2019 onwards, so you can see when movement started, whether it is accelerating and how it follows the seasons.

Validated against GNSS

Results are tied to and checked against permanent GNSS stations, and each delivery ships with a quality report that shows its own error statistics.

Infrastructure owners

Screen pipelines, roads, rail, levees and utilities for subsidence before it becomes a repair bill.

Engineering and construction

Baseline a site before works, and watch dewatering or tunnelling effects on the buildings around it.

Insurance and real estate

Understand ground-movement exposure across a portfolio with evidence, not assumptions.

How it works

Sentinel-1 satellites image the same place every 6 to 12 days. Tiny phase differences between passes reveal how the ground has moved.

  1. Radar archiveYears of Sentinel-1 acquisitions over your area.
  2. Scatterer analysisStable points and homogeneous areas are measured through time.
  3. GNSS tieMotion is expressed in a real reference frame and checked against GNSS.
  4. DeliveryPoints, time series and a quality report you can audit.
